# Channel Practice skill

Follow these steps in order. Paths are relative to this repository's root. In another project, copy `templates/` and `checklists/`, or point back here.

Maya Sen is a fictional teaching example in the human chapters. Her details, scripts and numbers must never be treated as facts about the user, or as real-world data. Use the blank sections of the worksheets, not any filled teaching example, unless the human explicitly asks for a demo of the template.

## 0. Load context (cheap, once)

1. Read `AGENTS.md` (rules).
2. Read `docs/platform-snapshot.md` for anything that may have changed. If today is on or after 1 February 2027, recheck YPP numbers on the live pages before using them.
3. Do not read every chapter. Open only the chapter for the job (table in `AGENTS.md`).

## 1. Channel (if new)

Walk `checklists/channel-setup.md`. Phone verification unlocks custom thumbnails and videos over 15 minutes; advanced features unlock related-video links and A/B tests (`docs/02-channel-setup.md`).

## 2. Plan

Fill `templates/video-plan.md` with the human: viewer, their question in their words, learner outcome ("After this video, you will be able to…"), assumed knowledge, proof on screen, five honest titles. Ideas come from real questions first (`docs/03-niche-and-ideas.md`).

## 3. Script

Long video: `templates/long-video-script.md`. The answer starts within 30 seconds, one introduction, a few parts, one "but what about…", a real ending (`docs/04-first-long-video.md`). Short: `templates/shorts-script.md`; must work with no sound and no context (`docs/06-hooks-and-retention.md`, `docs/07-shorts-and-long-form.md`).

## 4. Package

Walk `checklists/title-and-thumbnail.md`. Title accurate, under 100 characters, important words first. Thumbnail shows something that happens in the video. Legitbait yes, misleading no (`docs/05-titles-and-thumbnails.md`).

## 5. Pre-upload check

Walk `checklists/before-you-upload.md`. Shorts over 1 minute with any copyright claim are blocked.

## 6. Review

After upload, fill `templates/analytics-review.md` with **official** Studio numbers only, pairing them as in `docs/08-analytics.md`. One next change. Never recommend deleting a video out of embarrassment.

## 7. Honesty checks (every time)

- No invented stats, people, quotes or sources
- Creator practice is not a YouTube rule; self-reported numbers are not benchmarks
- No algorithm myths about frequency, length or posting time
- YPP: official thresholds only, with the date; no earnings estimates, brand deals or tax advice
- Do not treat the fictional teaching example in the human chapters as facts about the user
- No publishing or account actions unless the human asked
