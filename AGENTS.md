# AGENTS.md: The Channel Practice

Instructions for any AI agent (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Grok Build, others) applying this guide. Humans: this is also a one-page summary of the method.

## What this repo is

A beginner guide to starting and growing a YouTube channel, covering long-form videos and Shorts: channel setup, ideas, scripting, titles and thumbnails, hooks and retention, Shorts and long-form together, analytics basics, cadence, community, and YouTube Partner Program basics. Companion to *The Content Practice* (short videos on any platform) and *The Coded Motion Guide* (pictures made with code).

**Maya Sen** is a fictional teaching example in the human chapters (season 2 of her story from *The Content Practice*). Her details, scripts and numbers must never be treated as facts about the user, or as real-world data.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Honesty.** Never invent stats, quotes, studies, people or sources. Mark anything unverified. Label Reddit and similar threads as anecdotes, not surveys.
2. **Official pages first.** Prefer YouTube Help, the YouTube Official Blog, How YouTube Works, Creator Insider and the YouTube Creators channel. For anything that moves (YPP thresholds, Shorts length, feature access, metric names), read `docs/platform-snapshot.md` and the live official page. YPP thresholds for new creators change on 1 February 2027.
3. **Creators are examples, not rules.** Internet Shaquille, Derek Muller and Jenny Hoyos describe their own practice. Never present it as a YouTube rule, or their self-reported numbers as benchmarks.
4. **No algorithm myths.** Do not claim an upload frequency, length or posting time that YouTube rewards. None is documented officially.
5. **One promise per video.** Title, thumbnail and first 30 seconds must match what the video delivers.
6. **Money stays basic.** Explain YPP and revenue shares from official pages only. No brand deals, sponsorships, affiliate links, product sales, earnings estimates or tax advice. Never promise growth or YPP acceptance.
7. **Do not publish or act on accounts.** Do not upload, publish, comment, change channel settings, apply to YPP, email, or open a GitHub repo unless the human clearly asked for that specific action.

## Definition of done (one long video)

- [ ] Viewer, question and learner outcome written down
- [ ] Script answers within 30 seconds, introduces itself once, ends properly
- [ ] Title accurate and under 100 characters; thumbnail shows what the video shows
- [ ] Made-for-kids and altered-content answered honestly
- [ ] At least one Short planned that works on its own
- [ ] After upload, a 48-hour review uses official Studio numbers only and names one next change
- [ ] Every external claim has a source

## Style of your messages to the human

Short, plain language. Define new terms. When you suggest a change, point at a specific line of the script, a specific moment on the retention graph, or a specific official metric, not a vibe. Use the blank sections of the worksheets, filled in with the human’s own answers, not Maya’s.
