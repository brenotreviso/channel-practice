# Before you upload

For every long video and Short.

**Long video**

- [ ] Answer starts within 30 seconds; one introduction only
- [ ] Sound is clear on headphones and on a phone speaker
- [ ] Description: first lines say what the video is ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en))
- [ ] Chapters, if the video has parts: 00:00 first, at least 3, each 10 s or more ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en))
- [ ] End screen planned for the last 5–20 seconds ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789?hl=en))
- [ ] Two or three accurate hashtags at most ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en))

**Short**

- [ ] Square or vertical, up to 3 minutes ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en))
- [ ] Makes sense with no sound and no context
- [ ] If over 1 minute: no claimed music or clips, or it will be blocked (same page)
- [ ] Related video set, if I have advanced features ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en))

**Both**

- [ ] Made-for-kids answered honestly ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076?hl=en))
- [ ] Altered or synthetic content disclosed if realistic AI was used ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en))
- [ ] I own or have permission for everything in it (music, clips, images)
- [ ] Every fact I state on camera, I could source
