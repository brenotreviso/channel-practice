# 48-hour review

A first look, not a verdict. 48 hours is a **house rule**. Fill the [analytics review](../templates/analytics-review.md) as you tick. See [chapter 8](../docs/08-analytics.md).

- [ ] I wrote the promise of the video *before* opening Studio
- [ ] I used YouTube Studio only
- [ ] I noted impressions and CTR, and remembered that CTR is unreliable on small numbers and new videos ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154?hl=en))
- [ ] I noted average view duration or average percentage viewed
- [ ] I looked at the retention graph, or wrote a plain note if it is too new (key moments need 60 s and 100 views, [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en))
- [ ] I noted the top traffic source
- [ ] I read the comments for questions
- [ ] I wrote **one** change for the next video
- [ ] I did not compare this video to a much larger channel
- [ ] I did not delete or unlist it out of embarrassment
- [ ] I will look again at 7 days, not tonight
