# YPP readiness

Only when you are near a threshold. Recheck every number on the live pages first; thresholds for new creators change on **1 February 2027**. See [chapter 11](../docs/11-partner-program.md).

- [ ] I live in a country or region where YPP is available ([YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en))
- [ ] No active Community Guidelines strikes
- [ ] 2-Step Verification is on for my Google Account
- [ ] Advanced features are on ([feature access](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9890437?hl=en))
- [ ] I have, or will create in Studio, one AdSense for YouTube account
- [ ] I checked which level I am aiming at: 500 subscribers (fan funding, Shopping, [expanded YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=en)) or 1,000 subscribers (ad revenue, [YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en); from 1 Feb 2027, [new thresholds](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en))
- [ ] I am counting **qualified** watch hours (public long-form only) or **qualified** Shorts views (public, Shorts feed), not total views
- [ ] My channel is mostly original work; I have read the monetisation policies, including reused content ([Shorts monetisation policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en))
- [ ] I know a review typically takes about a month, and what to do if rejected (appeal in 21 days, or reapply after 30)
