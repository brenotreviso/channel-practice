# Title and thumbnail

Before every long-video upload. See [chapter 5](../docs/05-titles-and-thumbnails.md).

**Title**

- [ ] It accurately represents the video ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en))
- [ ] Important words come first; branding or episode numbers at the end
- [ ] Under 100 characters ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407?hl=en)); short enough to read when cut off
- [ ] Little or no ALL CAPS and emoji
- [ ] I decided on purpose: searchable or intriguing
- [ ] I wrote at least five versions before choosing

**Thumbnail**

- [ ] It shows something that really happens in the video
- [ ] One clear subject, readable at phone size
- [ ] Text (if any) adds to the title instead of repeating it
- [ ] 16:9, as large as possible (3840 × 2160 recommended), JPG or PNG ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en))
- [ ] Follows the Community Guidelines

**Together**

- [ ] A viewer who watched to the end would call the title and thumbnail fair
- [ ] The first 30 seconds deliver what they promise
