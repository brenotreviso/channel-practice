# Video plan

One page per video, before you write the script. See [chapter 3](../docs/03-niche-and-ideas.md) and [chapter 4](../docs/04-first-long-video.md).

## Maya’s plan (teaching example)

Maya Sen is fictional.

| Line | Maya’s answer |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Gets home tired, can cook a little, embarrassed when dinner tastes off |
| **Their question (in their words)** | “Why does my tomato pan taste bitter?” |
| **Where it came from** | Twelve comments on her Reels asking about the same dinner |
| **Learner outcome** | After this video, you will be able to cook the one-pan tomato dinner without the bitter taste |
| **What I assume they know** | How to chop an onion |
| **What they do not need** | Her week, a second recipe, the history of tomatoes |
| **Proof on screen** | Onions at 3 minutes vs 12 minutes, side by side |
| **Tone** | Calm, a bit funny about her own mistake |
| **Format** | Long video (4–6 min; aiming for ~5) + 2 Shorts |
| **Title ideas (5)** | Why your tomato dinner tastes bitter (it’s the onions) · Fix a bitter tomato sauce: one pan, ten minutes · The onion mistake that ruins tomato dinners · Bitter tomato pasta? Try this first · One-pan tomato dinner, without the bitterness |
| **Thumbnail idea** | Two spoons of onions, pale vs brown, “3 MIN vs 12 MIN” |
| **Shorts from it** | (1) the low-heat trick; (2) the salt-early tip |
| **Next video (end screen)** | What to do with the leftovers |
