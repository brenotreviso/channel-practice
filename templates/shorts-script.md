# Shorts script

For a 20–60 second Short. It must make sense to someone who has never seen your channel and has the sound off. See [chapter 6](../docs/06-hooks-and-retention.md) and [chapter 7](../docs/07-shorts-and-long-form.md). Shorts can be up to three minutes ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en)); shorter is easier for a beginner (house rule).

## Maya’s Short (teaching example)

Maya Sen is fictional.

| Beat | Time | On screen | Said / caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First frame (the hook)** | 0:00 | Two spoons of onions, pale vs brown, text: “3 min vs 12 min” | (no words for the first second) |
| **The point** | 0:01–0:10 | Pale onions in a pan on high heat | “This is why your tomato dinner tastes bitter.” |
| **The proof / steps** | 0:10–0:30 | Turn heat down, pinch of salt, lid on | “Low heat. Salt early. Lid on. Twelve minutes.” |
| **Payoff** | 0:30–0:38 | Finished pan, spoon in | “Sweet, not bitter.” |
| **Loop or end** | 0:38–0:40 | Cut back to the two spoons | (ends where it began) |

**Related video:** “Why your tomato dinner tastes bitter (it’s the onions)” (once she has advanced features).
**Music:** none, so nothing can be claimed.
**Title:** Why your tomato dinner tastes bitter
