# Long-video script

For a 4–6 minute video. The shape comes from [chapter 4](../docs/04-first-long-video.md): answer early, a few clear parts, one honest “but what about…”, a real ending. Timings are **house rules**.

## Maya’s script outline (teaching example)

Maya Sen is fictional. Lines are written for teaching, not from a real video.

| Part | Time | What she says / shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Open: restate the question and start answering** | 0:00–0:25 | On the pan: “If your tomato dinner tastes bitter, it’s probably the onions. Here’s the fix, then two ways to make it faster.” |
| **Part 1: the cause** | 0:25–1:30 | Two spoons side by side: 3 minutes on high vs 12 minutes on low. Taste test. |
| **Part 2: the fix** | 1:30–3:00 | Low heat, pinch of salt early, lid on for the first five minutes. Shows each step. |
| **Part 3: but what about…** | 3:00–4:30 | “But I don’t have twelve minutes.” Two faster options: slice thinner; cook a batch on Sunday and freeze. |
| **Ending** | 4:30–5:15 | “Tonight, just try the low heat. That’s it.” One sentence on leftovers; end screen to the leftovers video. |
| **Ask (after the promise)** | in the ending | “Tell me what you add to yours. I read all of them.” |

**Cut from draft 1:** 90 seconds about her week; a second recipe; “basically” ×6.
