# Upload calendar

A fortnight you can keep for three months. See [chapter 9](../docs/09-cadence.md). The cadence is a **house rule**; YouTube does not publish a required upload frequency.

## Maya’s fortnight (teaching example)

Maya Sen is fictional.

| Day | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Sun (wk 1) | Pick the question; fill the video plan; write and read the script | 2 h |
| Wed | Film long video, thumbnail stills, two vertical clips | 2 h |
| Sun (wk 2) | Edit; title, thumbnail, description, chapters, end screen | 3 h |
| Tue (wk 2) | Publish long video; reply to comments in the evening | 30 min |
| Thu (wk 2) | 48-hour look; publish Short 1 (linked once available) | 30 min |
| Mon (wk 3) | Publish Short 2 | 20 min |
| Tue (wk 3) | 7-day look; analytics review | 30 min |

**What she drops first when busy:** Short 2. **Never dropped:** the review.
