# 9 · Cadence and a week you can keep

> **In one sentence:** upload as often as you can while keeping each video good and yourself well, and keep the rhythm steady enough that viewers know what to expect.

## New words

| Word | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cadence** | How often you upload. |
| **Batching** | Doing the same kind of task for several videos in one sitting. |
| **Burnout** | Exhaustion that makes you stop. On YouTube, it usually ends the channel. |

## What we know about upload frequency

YouTube does not publish an upload frequency that its systems reward. Claims such as “daily uploads boost you” or “post three times a week or the algorithm forgets you” are **unverified** in this guide.

What has been said on the record:

- In a 2019 interview, **Matt Koval**, then leading YouTube’s official Creators channel, said it pained him to see creators raise their upload frequency just because they think it will grow their views. He described diminishing returns, and creators “uploading more and more and then their views are going down and down”; his advice when that happens is to slow down and put more effort into quality ([Video Creators, 15 Aug 2019](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0VUdByrNIK4)).
- In the same interview he asked creators to think about how often their audience actually watches, and said there may be exceptions (news, some gaming audiences). He also said YouTube’s own Creators channel published about once a week at the time. That was 2019 and one channel.
- YouTube’s Help suggests that to grow **regular viewers**, you upload consistent content about similar topics or in a familiar format ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10246996?hl=en-GB)). That is consistency of *what*, not a required *how often*.

The interview is on a third-party channel and is seven years old. Use it for its reasoning, not as current policy.

## Choose a cadence you can keep for three months

| Time you have | A sustainable start (house rule) |
|---|---|
| 2 evenings a week | 1 long video every 2 weeks, plus 1–2 Shorts cut from it |
| 3–4 evenings a week | 1 long video a week, plus 2 Shorts |
| Mostly weekends | 1 long video every 2 weeks, filmed on one weekend and edited the next |

Pick the slower option if you are unsure. You can speed up later; it is harder to recover from stopping.

## A fortnight, batched

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Sunday (week 1) | Pick the question. Write the plan and script. Read aloud |
| Wednesday | Film the long video, the thumbnail stills, and one or two vertical clips |
| Sunday (week 2) | Edit the long video. Title, thumbnail, description, chapters, end screen |
| Tuesday (week 2) | Publish the long video |
| Thursday (week 2) | 48-hour look. Publish Short 1, linked to the long video |
| Monday (week 3) | Publish Short 2, linked to the long video |
| Tuesday (week 3) | 7-day look; fill the review sheet |

Use the [upload calendar](templates/upload-calendar.md).

## If you are in the YouTube Partner Program later

Activity rules exist for channels already in YPP. Today, YouTube may turn off monetisation for channels that have not uploaded or posted for six months or more ([YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)). From **1 February 2027**, a YPP channel counts as active if it has 1,000 qualified watch hours in the past 365 days, or 1 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days, **or** two long-form videos or five Shorts uploaded every 90 days ([Changes to YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en)). Those are floors for monetised channels, not targets for beginners.

## Protecting the habit

- Keep a list of ten ideas so a blank week is never the reason you skip.
- Decide in advance what you will drop when life gets busy (a Short, not the review).
- Internet Shaquille points out that viewers are not watching as closely as you think, and that he would not post an apology for a missed month ([his process video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aBUy0z5HNlY)). If you miss an upload, upload the next one.

## Maya

Maya’s two-evening week from season 1 becomes a fortnight: one long video a fortnight and two Shorts cut from each long video, plus whatever she still posts on Instagram. When her job gets busy in September, she drops one Short, keeps the long video, and does not post an apology.
