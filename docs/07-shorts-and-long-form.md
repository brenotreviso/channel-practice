# 7 · Shorts and long-form together

> **In one sentence:** Shorts are a way for new people to meet you and long videos are where they get to know you; make each one complete on its own, then link them.

## New words

| Word | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Short** | A square or vertical video up to three minutes long. Since 15 October 2024, any square or vertical upload up to three minutes is treated as a Short ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en)). |
| **Shorts feed** | The swipeable vertical feed. |
| **Views (Shorts)** | Since 31 March 2025, counted each time a Short starts or replays, with no minimum watch time ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en)). Since November 2026, every format counts a view the moment it starts to play, and public counts include estimated co-views on TVs ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281?hl=en); see [chapter 8](08-analytics.md)). |
| **Engaged views** | The older Shorts view count: viewers who chose to keep watching. YPP eligibility and Shorts revenue still use engaged views (same page). |
| **Related video** | A link from a Short to another video of yours, shown under your handle ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en)). |

## How the two formats feed each other

What YouTube has said publicly:

- It runs **separate recommendation systems** for Shorts and long-form. They understand viewing across formats, but each focuses on its own. YouTube says it has not seen evidence of Shorts hurting long-form performance ([YouTube Blog, May 2025](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/debunking-common-myths-about-youtube-shorts/)).
- It describes the Shorts feed as a way to reach **new viewers** and introduce them to your content (same post).
- In January 2023, a YouTube vice-president wrote that channels uploading both Shorts and long-form were “seeing better overall watch time and subscriber growth” than channels uploading only long-form ([YouTube Blog, 24 Jan 2023](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/3-creator-economy-trends-2023/)). No method was given, and channels that do both may differ in other ways. Read it as YouTube’s observation, not proof that adding Shorts will grow *your* channel.

What that means in practice:

1. **Each Short must stand alone.** Someone swiping has not seen your long video. A Short that only says “watch the full video” gives them nothing.
2. **Link the long video as the Short’s related video** when you have advanced features ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en)). The link appears below your handle.
3. **Watch hours from Shorts in the Shorts feed do not count toward the 4,000 hours.** In YouTube’s words, “any qualified watch hours from Shorts views in the Shorts Feed won’t count towards the 4,000 qualified public watch hours threshold.” The two routes into the YouTube Partner Program are counted separately ([YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)). See [chapter 11](11-partner-program.md).
4. **Do not expect every Shorts viewer to watch long-form.** Some will, many will not. Judge your long videos by their own numbers.

## Making a Short from a long video

The cheapest Short is one moment from a long video you already made:

- Pick the one most visual, most useful moment. Not the intro.
- Reframe for vertical. If you know you will do this, film a vertical clip of that moment on the day.
- Open on the result or the problem, not a greeting ([chapter 6](06-hooks-and-retention.md)).
- Captions on screen; many people swipe with the sound off. (House rule, widely practised.)
- End on the payoff. Jenny Hoyos describes a good Short as one where you watch to the end and then rewatch ([interview, 2023](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=As7abwNhG7Y)).

Use the [Shorts script template](templates/shorts-script.md) for Shorts you film on purpose.

## Shorts-only, long-only, or both?

| Choice | Fits when | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Shorts only | You have very little time, or your ideas are single moments | Fewer chances to go deep; watch hours from Shorts in the Shorts feed do not count toward the 4,000 hours |
| Long-form only | Your topic needs steps, examples or explanation | Slower to make; harder for new viewers to find you at first |
| Both | You can make one long video and cut one or two Shorts from it | More editing; keep each complete |

For most beginners with a full-time job, “one long video, plus Shorts cut from it” is the least extra work. That is a house rule, not a YouTube recommendation.

## Maya

Maya’s Instagram habits make Shorts feel easy. The risk is that she drifts back to only Shorts because they are quicker. Her rule: every long video gets two Shorts cut from it, and each Short has to make sense to a stranger. Once her channel has advanced features, each Short links to its long video. Mending clips and canal walks stay as stand-alone Shorts while she tests whether those pillars belong on YouTube at all.
