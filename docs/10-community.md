# 10 · Community

> **In one sentence:** the people who comment are the start of your returning audience, so reply like a person, ask for things that help them, and give them somewhere to go next.

## New words

| Word | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pinned comment** | A comment you fix at the top of a video’s comments ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9482367?hl=en)). |
| **Heart** | A creator’s small “thank you” on a comment (same page). |
| **Posts** | Text, image, poll, quiz, GIF or video posts on your channel’s Posts tab ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9409631?hl=en)). |
| **End screen** | Clickable elements in the last 5–20 seconds of a long video ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789?hl=en)). |

## Comments

In Studio or the Studio app you can reply, heart, like, pin, remove, report spam, or hide a user from your channel ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9482367?hl=en)). You can also hold comments for review and set channel guidelines ([comment settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9483359?hl=en)).

House rules for a new channel:

- Reply to every genuine comment for the first months. It is the cheapest research you will get.
- Pin a comment that asks the viewer something useful (“What do you add to yours?”). It starts the next video.
- Hide or remove abuse without arguing. Your comment section is not a debate club.
- Turn questions into videos ([chapter 3](03-niche-and-ideas.md)), and say so: “Asha asked this under my last video.” (Ask first, or use their first name only if they are happy to be named.)

YouTube says Shorts can build community too, by responding to comments ([YouTube Blog, May 2025](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/debunking-common-myths-about-youtube-shorts/)).

## Asking for things

Internet Shaquille’s distinction is useful: asking for subscribers so you reach a number is for you; inviting viewers to say what you missed or got wrong also serves them ([his process video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aBUy0z5HNlY)). Both are allowed. Keep asks few, and put them after you have delivered the promise, not before.

## Posts

You can post polls, quizzes, GIFs, text, music, images and video. Posts appear on your channel (Posts shelf on the Home tab, and the Posts tab), and may show on viewers’ homepages and Subscriptions feed; image posts may also appear in the Shorts feed. Subscribers may get a notification ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9409631?hl=en)).

Use them sparingly and usefully: a poll to choose the next video, a photo from the next shoot, a correction. If you later join the YouTube Partner Program, posting to the Posts tab also counts as activity under the current rules ([YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)).

## Give them somewhere to go next

- **End screens:** up to four elements, last 5–20 seconds, video at least 25 seconds; you can show a specific video, a playlist, a subscribe button, or another channel. Not available on videos set as made for kids ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789?hl=en)).
- **Playlists and series:** group videos that answer related questions, so a viewer who finishes one has an obvious next one.
- **Shorts related video:** link a Short to the long video it came from ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en)).

## Look after yourself

Comments are not a measure of your worth. One rude comment can feel louder than twenty kind ones. Decide in advance what you will delete without reading twice, and who you will show the hard ones to.
