# The Channel Practice

**Start a YouTube channel from nothing, make your first long video and your first Short, and learn the basics of growing it, without guessing what “the algorithm” wants.**

This guide is for complete beginners to YouTube. It covers long-form videos and Shorts, and how the two work together. Unlike *The Content Practice*, which leaves growth out on purpose, this guide does cover growing an audience on YouTube: being found, being clicked, being watched, and being come back to. Every term is defined. Every external claim has a linked source, checked on 22 November 2026. Anything we could not confirm is marked **unverified**. Creator advice is labelled as one creator’s practice, not a rule. Reddit threads are labelled **anecdotes**, not surveys.

The worked example is **[Maya Sen](meet-maya.md)**, the fictional beginner from *The Content Practice*, in a second season. In season 1 she posted on Instagram only and chose not to open TikTok yet; she added TikTok later, and both are now going steadily. Now she is trying long-form YouTube, with Shorts alongside. She is not a real person.

## What this guide is not

It is not an “algorithm hack” list, and it does not promise growth. Chapter 11 is a short departure from *The Content Practice*’s no-money stance: it explains where the YouTube Partner Program line is, because beginners ask, and why it is not a first-year goal. Brand deals, sponsorships, affiliate links and selling products are out of scope. We are not recommending a course, tool or paid program.
