# Glossary

Short definitions used in this guide. If a chapter and this page ever disagree, prefer the chapter and the official page it links, then file a correction.

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **A/B test** | Showing up to three titles or thumbnails to different viewers at once; YouTube picks the winner by watch time share. |
| **AdSense for YouTube** | The Google account YouTube uses to pay partners. |
| **Advanced features** | The top level of YouTube feature access, from channel history or ID/video verification. Needed for related video links, A/B tests and YPP. |
| **Audience retention** | A graph of how many viewers are still watching at each moment. |
| **Average percentage viewed** | Average share of a video watched among those who stayed to watch. |
| **Average view duration (AVD)** | Average minutes watched among those who stayed to watch. |
| **Batching** | Doing the same kind of task for several videos in one sitting. |
| **Brand Account** | A channel that can use a different name from your Google Account and have several managers. |
| **Browse** | Viewers finding you without searching: Home, Up Next, Subscriptions, the Shorts feed. |
| **Cadence** | How often you upload. |
| **Call to action (CTA)** | A next step you invite the viewer to take. |
| **Channel** | Your public presence on YouTube. |
| **Chapters** | Timestamped sections of a video, listed in the description. |
| **Click-through rate (CTR)** | Of the times your thumbnail was shown on YouTube, the share that became a view. |
| **Clickbait** | Packaging that wins the click and then disappoints. |
| **Co-views** | YouTube’s estimate of several people watching together on a TV. Included in public view counts since November 2026. |
| **Creator Pool** | The share of Shorts Feed ad revenue divided among monetising Shorts creators. |
| **End screen** | Clickable elements in the last 5–20 seconds of a long video. |
| **Engaged views** | Views where someone stayed past the first seconds. Used for YPP and Shorts revenue. |
| **External traffic** | Views from websites and apps outside YouTube that link to or embed your video. |
| **Fan funding** | Channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks. |
| **Feature access** | YouTube’s standard, intermediate and advanced levels of tools. |
| **Handle** | Your unique @name on YouTube. |
| **Hook** | The opening that tells a viewer they are in the right place and why to stay. |
| **House rule** | A rule of thumb from this guide, labelled as such. Not a platform rule. |
| **Impression** | Your thumbnail was shown to someone on YouTube (a registered impression). |
| **Intro (Studio)** | Share of viewers still watching after the first 30 seconds. |
| **Learner outcome** | What the viewer can do after watching. |
| **Long-form video** | A regular YouTube video, usually horizontal, watched on the watch page. |
| **Made for kids** | A required audience setting under COPPA and an FTC agreement. |
| **Monthly audience** | Estimated unique viewers in the last 28 days. |
| **New / casual / regular viewers** | First time in the period / watched at least monthly for 1–5 of the last 12 months / for more than 6. |
| **Packaging** | Title plus thumbnail. |
| **Pillar** | A repeating theme you can make many videos about. |
| **Posts** | Text, image, poll, quiz or video posts on your channel’s Posts tab. |
| **Qualified watch hours** | Hours watched on your public long-form videos, counted for YPP. |
| **Qualified Shorts views** | Views of your public Shorts in the Shorts feed, counted for YPP. |
| **Related video** | A link from a Short to another of your videos, shown under your handle. |
| **Returning viewers** | People who had watched your channel before and came back in the period. |
| **Short** | A square or vertical video up to three minutes long. |
| **Shorts feed** | The swipeable vertical feed where most Shorts are watched. |
| **Stayed to watch** | For Shorts: share of times viewers stayed past the first seconds. |
| **Thumbnail** | The still image shown before someone clicks a long video. |
| **Traffic source** | How a viewer found your video. |
| **Unverified** | We looked and could not confirm it from a primary source. |
| **YouTube Studio** | YouTube’s official dashboard for uploads, settings and analytics. |
| **YPP** | YouTube Partner Program. |
