# 2 · Set up your channel

> **In one sentence:** spend one evening on the settings that block you later (verification, audience, description), and none on the ones that do not (banner art, a logo, a trailer).

## New words

| Word | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Handle** | Your short, unique @name. It is different from your channel name ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11585688?hl=en)). |
| **Brand Account** | A channel that can use a name other than your Google Account’s, and can have more than one owner or manager ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1646861?hl=en)). |
| **Feature access** | YouTube’s three levels of tools: standard, intermediate and advanced ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9890437?hl=en)). |
| **Made for kids** | A setting you must answer for every channel or video, required under an agreement with the US Federal Trade Commission and COPPA, wherever you live ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076?hl=en)). |

## Step 1 · Create the channel

From YouTube **Settings**, choose **Add or manage channel(s)**, then **Create a channel**, and pick a picture, a name and a handle. Use the Brand Account route if you want a different public name, or co-managers later ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1646861?hl=en)).

**Name.** Use the name people will remember from your other accounts, if you have them. You can change it later. A name is not a strategy.

## Step 2 · Unlock the features you will need

| Level | How you get it | What it unlocks (examples) |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | Creating a channel (no active Community Guidelines strikes) | Uploading videos and Shorts, playlists |
| **Intermediate** | Verify your phone number | Videos longer than 15 minutes, custom thumbnails, live streaming from a computer |
| **Advanced** | Channel history, or verify with ID or a video | Clickable links in long-video descriptions, A/B testing titles and thumbnails, extra channels, Content ID appeals |

Source: [Feature access for YouTube creators](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9890437?hl=en). Several features later in this guide need **advanced** access, including Shorts **related video** links ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en)) and A/B tests ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714?hl=en)). Chapters need it if you do not already have chapters access ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en)). Check yours in Studio: **Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility**.

Verify your phone today. Without it you cannot add a custom thumbnail ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en)).

## Step 3 · Write a two-line description

Line 1: who the channel is for. Line 2: what they get, and how often, if you know. That is enough. You will rewrite it after ten videos.

## Step 4 · Set your upload defaults once

Studio has a **Settings → Upload defaults** page, with advanced settings such as automatic chapters ([chapters Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en)). Whether you set them there or per video, decide these once:

- **Audience (made for kids).** Answer honestly. A cooking channel for adults is not “made for kids” just because it is friendly. Read YouTube’s page if you are unsure ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076?hl=en)). Videos set as made for kids lose some features, such as end screens ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789?hl=en)).
- **Altered or synthetic content.** If you use generative AI to make something realistic (a real person saying something they did not, altered footage of a real event, a realistic scene that did not happen), you must disclose it at upload ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)). The same page says disclosure alone will not limit a video’s audience or its eligibility to earn.
- **Comments.** Decide whether comments are held for review. You can change this per video ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9483359?hl=en)).

## What to skip for now

Banner art, a logo, a channel trailer, a “welcome” video, and a 30-video content plan. None of them help a viewer who has never heard of you. Your first video does.

## Maya

Maya creates a Brand Account channel with her own name, so a friend can help moderate comments later. She verifies her phone the same evening. Her description:

> Weeknight cooking for people who get home tired. One pan, real mistakes, about one new video a fortnight.

She answers “not made for kids”, leaves comments open, and does not make a banner.
