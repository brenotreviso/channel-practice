## Unverified or limited items (read before quoting)

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| YPP thresholds | Correct on the official pages on 22 Nov 2026. **New-creator thresholds change on 1 February 2027** (8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views, plus 1,000 subscribers). Recheck after that date. |
| “3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days” for the 500-subscriber level | Stated on the [expanded YPP page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=en); not repeated on the [February 2027 changes page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en). Kept as currently written. |
| “90% of the best-performing videos have custom thumbnails” | YouTube’s own statement on [Thumbnail & title tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en). No method or date given. |
| Channels uploading Shorts and long-form see “better overall watch time and subscriber growth” | YouTube’s own observation in a [January 2023 blog post](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/3-creator-economy-trends-2023/). No method given; correlational at best. |
| “No evidence of Shorts negatively impacting long-form” | YouTube’s own statement ([blog, May 2025](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/debunking-common-myths-about-youtube-shorts/)). Not independently tested. |
| Any upload frequency, length or posting time “the algorithm” rewards | **Unverified.** No official page we opened gives one. |
| Matt Koval on upload frequency | A 2019 interview on a third-party channel, when he led YouTube’s Creators channel. Reasoning only; not current policy. |
| Jenny Hoyos’s retention and swipe figures, and her “fifth-grade” readability target | **Self-reported** in a 2023 podcast interview; her own method. Not benchmarks, not verified. |
| Jenny Hoyos’s “one second to hook” | Her opinion, said on YouTube’s Creator Insider channel (Dec 2024). Published by YouTube, but not a YouTube rule or measurement. |
| Internet Shaquille’s time split (about 40% on endings) and “sound matters more than picture” | His own estimate and opinion, stated in his video. Not measured. |
| Derek Muller’s view counts and examples | From his 2021 video about his own channel. Not re-checked against current counts. |
| Using “YouTube” in this guide’s name | The rule we read is in YouTube’s **developer** (API) [branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/branding-guidelines), which cover apps, products and services. YouTube’s consumer brand site ([brand.youtube](https://brand.youtube/)) is script-rendered and could not be read; whether the same rule covers a free guide is **unverified**. |
| YouTube Studio names and menus | YouTube says an updated Studio is rolling out from July 2026 ([Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11962757?hl=en)). Menu names may differ. |
| Reddit threads | **Anecdotes**, not prevalence data. Opened for *The Content Practice* on 20 Nov 2026; Reddit returned 403 to an automated check on 22 Nov 2026, so they were not re-opened. |
| Maya Sen, her comments, scripts and “analytics” numbers | **Fictional** teaching examples. Not a real person or real Studio data. |
| House rules (4–6 minute first video, 30-second answer, 48 h / 7 d / 28 d reviews, fortnightly cadence) | Our rules of thumb, labelled as such. Not YouTube rules. |
