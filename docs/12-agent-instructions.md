# 12 · Agent instructions

> **In one sentence:** an AI agent using this guide should help a human plan, script, package, check and review YouTube videos, without inventing stats, overstating what YouTube has said, or treating Maya as a real person.

The full rules are in [`AGENTS.md`](https://github.com/brenotreviso/channel-practice/blob/main/AGENTS.md) and the step-by-step [`channel-practice` skill](https://github.com/brenotreviso/channel-practice/blob/main/.agents/skills/channel-practice/SKILL.md). This page is the human-readable summary.

## What an agent should do

- Load `AGENTS.md`, then the [platform snapshot](platform-snapshot.md). Open only the chapter for the job.
- Help fill the **blank** sections of the worksheets with the human’s own answers.
- Draft a video plan, a long-video script or a Shorts script from the templates, with one promise and an early answer.
- Suggest several honest titles and thumbnail ideas, and check them against the [title and thumbnail checklist](checklists/title-and-thumbnail.md).
- Read official YouTube Studio numbers the human shares, pair them ([chapter 8](08-analytics.md)), and suggest **one** next change.
- Recheck anything time-sensitive (YPP thresholds, Shorts length, feature access) on the live official page before stating it.

## What an agent must not do

- Invent statistics, quotes, studies, people or sources.
- Treat **Maya Sen** as a real creator, client or source.
- Present a creator’s personal practice (Internet Shaquille, Derek Muller, Jenny Hoyos) as a YouTube rule, or their self-reported numbers as benchmarks.
- Claim an upload frequency, video length or posting time that “the algorithm” rewards. None is documented officially.
- Promise growth, views, or YPP acceptance.
- Advise on brand deals, sponsorships, affiliate links, selling products, or tax.
- Upload, publish, comment, apply to YPP, change channel settings, or create accounts unless the human clearly asked for that specific action.

## Definition of done (one long video)

- [ ] Viewer, question and learner outcome written down
- [ ] Script answers the promise within 30 seconds and has a real ending
- [ ] Title is accurate, under 100 characters, important words first
- [ ] Thumbnail matches what the video shows
- [ ] Made-for-kids and altered-content questions answered honestly
- [ ] At least one Short planned, complete on its own
- [ ] After upload, a 48-hour review uses official Studio numbers only and names one next change
- [ ] Every external claim has a source

## How this sits with the companion guides

For short videos on other platforms, follow *The Content Practice* `AGENTS.md`. For pictures made with code, follow *The Coded Motion Guide*. This guide covers YouTube: channel, long-form, Shorts, packaging, analytics and YPP basics.
