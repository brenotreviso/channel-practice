# 11 · The Partner Program, and why it isn’t your goal yet

> **In one sentence:** the YouTube Partner Program (YPP) is how channels earn from YouTube itself; know roughly where the line is, then go back to making videos, because none of this is a first-year goal.

**A departure from season 1.** *The Content Practice* left money out on purpose. This guide keeps one short chapter on it, because YouTube’s own program shapes what beginners hear about watch hours and Shorts views, and it is better to read the rules once than to guess. It covers only YouTube’s own program. Brand deals, sponsorships, affiliate links, selling products and tax are out of scope. Nothing here is financial advice.

!!! warning "Refresh after 1 February 2027"
    New entry thresholds for **new** creators start on 1 February 2027 ([Changes to YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en)). This chapter and the [Platform snapshot](platform-snapshot.md) must be rechecked after that date. Recheck the live pages before you quote any number.

## New words

| Word | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **YPP** | YouTube Partner Program: access to monetisation features and Creator Support, and a share of ad revenue ([YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)). |
| **Qualified watch hours** | Hours watched on your **public long-form** videos. Not private, unlisted or deleted videos, ads, or Shorts (same page). |
| **Qualified Shorts views** | Views of your public Shorts in the Shorts feed (same page). |
| **Fan funding** | Channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers and Super Thanks ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13195878?hl=en)). |

## What you need to join

From the [YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en): follow the channel monetisation policies; live where YPP is available; have no active Community Guidelines strikes; turn on 2-Step Verification; have **advanced features** ([chapter 2](02-channel-setup.md)); and link one AdSense for YouTube account.

## Thresholds today (checked 22 November 2026)

- **500 subscribers** (expanded YPP, in listed countries including the UK and US): fan funding and Shopping. Plus 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months or 3 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days ([Expanded YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=en)).
- **1,000 subscribers**: ad revenue sharing. Plus either 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months or 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days ([YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)).

Watch hours from Shorts views in the Shorts feed do **not** count toward the 4,000 qualified public watch hours (same page). Ad revenue also requires being at least 18, or a guardian over 18 who handles payments ([How to earn money on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en)).

**Unverified detail:** the expanded-YPP page lists the 3 uploads; the February 2027 changes page restates the 500-subscriber level without them. We keep the condition as currently written.

## What changes from 1 February 2027

From [Changes to the YouTube Partner Program](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en):

- **New creators** joining for ad revenue will need 1,000 subscribers and 8,000 qualified watch hours in the last 365 days, or 20 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days.
- **Channels already in YPP:** “Your status is not impacted by this update.”
- **Fan funding and Shopping:** no change to the 500-subscriber level.

Shorts pool, Premium Lite, targeted Shorts ads, activity rules and the terms deadline are on the [Platform snapshot](platform-snapshot.md).

## How the money is split, in brief

Partners get **55%** of net revenue from ads on their public long videos, **45%** of their Shorts Creator Pool allocation, and **70%** of fan funding ([earnings overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902?hl=en)). YouTube says the agreement gives no guarantee “about how much or whether you’ll be paid” (same page). We do not quote earnings per view: no official page we opened gives one.

## Applying

Apply in Studio under **Earn** once eligible. Meeting a threshold is not automatic entry: YouTube reviews the channel **as a whole**, and a decision typically takes about a month. If rejected, appeal within 21 days or reapply after 30 days ([YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)).

## Why it isn’t your goal yet

- A first-year channel with a full-time job should not plan around YPP. Plan around one good video at a time.
- Make **original** work. The review looks at the whole channel, and non-original Shorts views do not earn ([Shorts monetisation policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)).
- If you want a number to watch, watch returning viewers ([chapter 8](08-analytics.md)), not watch hours.
