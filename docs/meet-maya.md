# Meet Maya Sen: season 2

Maya Sen is the **fictional** beginner from *The Content Practice*. She is not a real person, client or data point. Her scripts, titles and analytics notes in this guide are teaching examples. Agents and teachers must not cite her as if she existed.

## Previously

In season 1 ([The Content Practice](https://brenotreviso.github.io/content-practice/meet-maya/)) Maya was 26, living in a shared flat in Manchester and working full-time in university administration. The year before, she had unpublished a few quiet posts rather than deleting them. She started again with one umbrella sentence and four pillars (weeknight cooking, clothes that last, short walks in the city, a notebook made a little public), posted short videos on Instagram only (she chose not to open TikTok, YouTube or X yet), filmed her hands and voice before her face, and ran a two-evening week. Her rule was: no view targets in month one, and no deleting.

## Season 2, in one paragraph

After season 1, once the Instagram habit held, she added TikTok, posting the same kind of short videos. A few months on, the Reels and TikToks have **traction**: strangers comment, some come back, and the same questions keep arriving (“how long do the onions take?”, “can you show the whole thing?”, “what do you do with the leftovers?”). Thirty seconds is not enough to answer them. So Maya decides to try **long-form YouTube**, with **Shorts** alongside. She is a beginner again: she has never made a video longer than a minute, never designed a thumbnail, and has never opened YouTube Studio. She still has a full-time job and the same flat.

We give no follower counts for her other accounts. “Traction” here means a small, steady audience, not a big one. Her numbers would not transfer to YouTube anyway (see [chapter 1](01-how-youtube-finds-viewers.md)).

## What she brings, and what she does not

| She already has | She does not have yet |
|---|---|
| A clear umbrella and four pillars | A long-video structure |
| The habit of one promise per video | Titles and thumbnails that work in a browse feed |
| Hands-and-voice filming, and some talking to camera | Comfort talking for five minutes |
| A 48-hour review habit | Any idea what CTR or average view duration mean |
| An audience elsewhere that asks questions | A reason for YouTube to show her to anyone yet |

## Why she is built this way

Her stalls in season 2 are taken from sources we opened, not invented. Forum posts are **anecdotes**: they show a worry is *said*, not how common it is.

| Stall | How it shows up for Maya | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| A long video feels like too much work next to a job | She plans one long video every two weeks, not every week | [r/socialmedia, advice that assumes eight hours a day](https://www.reddit.com/r/socialmedia/comments/1obkzro/im_tired_of_advice_that_assumes_everyone_has_8/); a YouTube employee on burnout and upload frequency ([Video Creators interview, 2019](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0VUdByrNIK4)) | Anecdote; interview |
| Thumbnails feel like clickbait | She learns the difference between an honest, enticing title and a misleading one | [Veritasium, “Clickbait is Unreasonably Effective” (2021)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S2xHZPH5Sng) | Creator video |
| Saying hello for a minute before starting | Her first draft opens with her week; viewers leave at 0:40 | [Internet Shaquille on “saying hi twice” (2023)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aBUy0z5HNlY) | Creator video |
| Quiet numbers feel like a verdict | She keeps the no-deleting rule from season 1 | [r/NewTubers, almost no views](https://www.reddit.com/r/NewTubers/comments/1p26dtm/how_do_you_keep_going_when_your_videos_get_almost/) | Anecdote |
| Comparing herself to huge cooking channels | She compares a video only to her own earlier videos | [Creator-friendly Algorithms (PDF, 2023)](https://pages.ischool.utexas.edu/hai-files/files/publications/74/2023-creator_friendly_algorithms.pdf), a study of existing YouTube creators | Academic paper |
| “Can I earn from this?” | She reads the official YPP pages once, and does not make it a goal | [YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en); [Changes to YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en) | Official |

Maya’s name, age, city and job are invented so the case study can stay specific. Her comments, titles and numbers are written for teaching.
