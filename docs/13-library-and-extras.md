## What this guide does not cover

Live streaming, podcasts on YouTube, YouTube Shopping, brand deals and sponsorships, affiliate links, selling your own products, music rights in depth, multi-language channels, and tax. Chapter 11 explains YPP thresholds and revenue shares and stops there. We are not recommending a next course, tool or programme.

## For teachers

Students can hand in the filled video plan, the script, the uploaded video (or an unlisted draft), and a ticked checklist. Maya is a shared character they may copy; their own plan must be theirs. There is no marking scheme for views or subscribers.
